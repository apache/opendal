Register the retry layer with an operator registry.

jitter accepts true, false, on, and off, ignoring ASCII case. factor must be finite and at least 1. min_delay and max_delay must be positive durations such as 500ms or PT1S. max_times is the maximum number of retries after the first attempt and accepts 0. Omitted options keep the defaults from RetryLayer::new.
