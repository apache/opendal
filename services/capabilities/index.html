About service capabilities

This matrix summarizes the native capabilities declared by each storage service implementation in OpenDAL. Keep the following points in mind when choosing a backend or relying on optional operations:

  • Native declarations vs. client-side fallbacks: Columns reflect native backend support. For example, list_with_recursive indicates native prefix or recursive listing supported by the backend itself (which may still paginate across requests), rather than client-side directory-by-directory traversal. Depending on the operator setup and language binding, OpenDAL can also simulate operations such as suffix byte-range reads, nested directory traversal for recursive listing or deletion, and empty directory markers on top of primitive operations.
  • Language binding surface: Each binding exposes its own idiomatic API and subset of options and capability checks. Check your language's guide in the documentation sidebar to confirm which options and inspection APIs are available.
  • Compatible storage endpoints: Self-hosted or protocol-compatible servers (such as S3-compatible object stores or WebDAV servers) may support only a subset of the capabilities and limits declared for the protocol. Verify optional conditions and batch or multipart limits against your target endpoint.
